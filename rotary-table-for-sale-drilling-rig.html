Frequently Asked Questions

What is a rotary table?

A rotary table is a key component of drilling rigs that allows for the rotation of the drill string to penetrate the earth's surface.

How do I know which rotary table is right for my rig?

The right rotary table depends on your specific rig setup, the depth of the wells you are drilling, and the type of materials you are working with. Our team at Oil Rigs Now can help you determine the best option.
