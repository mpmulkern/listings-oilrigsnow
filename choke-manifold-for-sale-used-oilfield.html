What is a Choke Manifold?

A choke manifold is a crucial component in oilfield operations, used to control the flow of fluids from a well. It allows operators to regulate pressure and manage the flow rate, ensuring safe and efficient drilling processes. At Oil Rigs Now, we stock both new and used choke manifolds, providing you with options that fit your budget and operational requirements.
