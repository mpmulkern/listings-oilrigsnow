Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is a hook block used for in drilling rigs?

A hook block is a crucial component of drilling rigs, used to connect the drilling line to the load. It helps to lift and lower the drill string and other heavy equipment during operations.

How do I know which hook block is right for my rig?
