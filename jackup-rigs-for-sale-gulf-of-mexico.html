Frequently Asked Questions

What is a jackup rig?

A jackup rig is a mobile platform used for offshore drilling. It consists of a buoyant hull that can be raised above the water's surface by extending legs down to the seabed.

How do I choose the right jackup rig?

Consider factors like water depth, drilling requirements, and specific project needs. Our experts can help guide your decision based on these criteria.
