Frequently Asked Questions

What is a rotary hose?

A rotary hose is a high-pressure hose used in drilling operations to transport drilling fluids from the surface to the drill bit.

How do I know what size rotary hose I need?

The size of the rotary hose depends on your drilling rig specifications and the type of fluid being transported. Consult our team for personalized recommendations.
