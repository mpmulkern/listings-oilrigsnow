What Are Accumulator Units?

Accumulator units are essential components in hydraulic systems used in oilfield operations. They store energy in the form of pressurized fluid, providing a reserve that can be utilized during peak demand. This capability enhances the efficiency and reliability of drilling rigs and other oilfield equipment.
