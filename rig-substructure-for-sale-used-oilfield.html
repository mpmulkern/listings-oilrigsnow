Frequently Asked Questions

What is a rig substructure?

A rig substructure is a crucial component of drilling rigs that provides support and stability during drilling operations. It elevates the rig to a suitable height and houses essential equipment.

How do I assess the quality of used rig substructures?

Quality assessment involves checking for structural integrity, wear and tear, and maintenance history. Our team can provide detailed inspections for your peace of mind.
