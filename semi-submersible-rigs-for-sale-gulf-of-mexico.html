Frequently Asked Questions

What is a semi-submersible rig?

A semi-submersible rig is a floating platform that is anchored to the seabed, designed to remain stable in deep water while drilling for oil or gas.

How deep can semi-submersible rigs operate?

Semi-submersible rigs can operate in water depths ranging from 1,000 to over 10,000 feet, making them suitable for deep-water drilling.

What are the advantages of using semi-submersible rigs?

They offer enhanced stability, safety, and efficiency, especially in rough sea conditions, allowing for uninterrupted drilling activities.
