Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is a swivel in drilling operations?

A swivel is a critical component that allows for the rotation of the drill string while preventing the drilling fluid from leaking out. It connects the drilling rig to the drill pipe and helps maintain the flow of drilling fluids.

How do I choose the right swivel for my rig?

When selecting a swivel, consider the load capacity, compatibility with your rig, and the specific drilling conditions. Our experts at Oil Rigs Now can help guide you through the selection process.
