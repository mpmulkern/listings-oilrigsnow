Frequently Asked Questions

What is a workover rig?

A workover rig is used for maintenance or repair of existing oil wells. It can also be utilized for drilling new wells.

How do I choose the right workover rig?

Consider factors such as the depth of the wells, the type of oil being extracted, and the specific features you need in a rig.
